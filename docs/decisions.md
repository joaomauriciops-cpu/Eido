\# Product Decisions

\- Mobile-first

\- Capture-first UX

\- Audio-first interaction

\- AI-assisted organization

\- Timeline-based memory

\- Minimal manual organization

\- Calm executive-assistant tone

\- Subscription business model

\- B2C first

\- over-engineered memory systems

\- Obsidian-like manual organization

\- Knowledge graph-first UX

\- Chat-first interaction model
