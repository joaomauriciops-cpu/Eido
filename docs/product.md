\# Eido Product Definition



\## Core Product



Eido is a mobile-first cognitive capture and execution assistant.



Users rapidly capture:

\- thoughts

\- conversations

\- meetings

\- obligations

\- ideas

\- reminders



The system:

\- transcribes

\- structures

\- extracts actionable items

\- creates continuity



\---



\## MVP Core Flow



1\. User records audio

2\. Audio is transcribed

3\. AI extracts:

&#x20;  - tasks

&#x20;  - events

&#x20;  - ideas

&#x20;  - notes

4\. User reviews results

5\. Eido saves and organizes



\---



\## Primary Emotional Outcomes



Users should feel:

\- clarity

\- calm

\- continuity

\- trust

\- reduced cognitive overload



\---



\## Eido Is NOT



\- a chatbot

\- a PKM system

\- a note-taking app

\- a productivity-maxxing tool

\- a social platform



\---



\## Core Differentiator



Eido transforms cognitive chaos into executable continuity.
